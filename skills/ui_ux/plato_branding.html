Plato — Brand Identity & Internal Design Hub

Brand identity design and SharePoint-based internal hub for Plato, a SaaS product at MindBridges.

Software Used

SharePoint | Photoshop

Overview

Plato is a SaaS product developed by MindBridges, designed to streamline project workflows and knowledge management for enterprise teams. As part of my role as UI/UX and Multimedia Designer, I was responsible for two interconnected workstreams: building the complete brand identity for Plato's market presence, and designing an internal brand hub on SharePoint that gave the global MindBridges team a centralised, always-accessible reference for brand standards, design assets, and product documentation.

The Challenge

Launching a new SaaS product means establishing brand credibility from zero. Plato needed a visual identity strong enough to instil confidence in enterprise buyers from the first touchpoint — logo, colour, typography, and tone all had to signal maturity and professionalism before the product had a track record. In parallel, the growing MindBridges team needed a practical solution for maintaining brand consistency across distributed contributors. Ad hoc file-sharing and informal brand communication was creating inconsistency in client-facing materials, which was a risk for a company working with global enterprise clients.

My Role

I led the full brand identity design for Plato — logo system, colour palette, typography, and brand usage guidelines. I then designed and built the SharePoint-based internal hub from scratch, structuring it to function both as a brand reference and a practical working environment for the team. This included designing the SharePoint pages, creating master asset libraries, and authoring the brand documentation that gave contributors clear, unambiguous guidance on how to use the Plato brand correctly.

Design Decisions

The Plato brand identity was built around a clean, confident visual language — a mark that communicated clarity and intelligence without relying on trends that would date quickly. For the SharePoint hub, usability was the primary design constraint: the structure needed to be intuitive enough that contributors could find what they needed without training, and flexible enough to scale as the brand and product evolved. Pages were designed with a clear information hierarchy, prominent asset download sections, and inline do/don't examples to reduce brand misuse without requiring a dedicated brand team to police it.

SharePoint Design

The internal SharePoint hub was designed to serve as a living brand system — not a static PDF. Each section of the hub corresponds to a pillar of the Plato brand: identity guidelines, asset library, product design principles, and communications standards. The layout prioritises quick navigation and rapid asset access, ensuring contributors can get what they need in seconds rather than searching through shared drives. The design language of the hub itself mirrors the Plato brand, so every interaction with the tool reinforces the visual standards it documents.

Outcome

Delivered a cohesive brand identity that gave Plato a confident, unified presence from its market launch. The SharePoint hub became the single source of truth for brand and design assets across the MindBridges team — reducing inconsistency in client-facing materials and streamlining onboarding for new contributors. The system was designed to scale, allowing new brand elements to be added without restructuring the hub's architecture.
