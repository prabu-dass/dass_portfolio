The designer who also animates the logo

I'm Prabu Dass. I started with posters and promo videos in college, and never picked just one discipline.

At Log2Base2 the founder asked for a 2D ad. I pitched a 3D one instead, a gorilla who learns to code, and spent almost three months learning Blender properly to make it. It became the company's flagship ad. That's roughly how I work: say yes to the harder version, then do the homework.

These days most of my time goes into product UX, but the graphic and motion work keeps my interface design honest. I know what an icon has to survive at 16 pixels, and how long a transition can last before it gets in the way.
