Making Mr. Kong

The brief was a 2D ad. I asked to make it in 3D, with a gorilla who learns to code. The founder said "okay", and that was the whole approval.

It was my first full Blender project, and there was no 3D team to lean on. Planning, sketching, modelling, rigging, lighting, rendering and the voice dubbing took almost three months. I wasn't a 3D expert when I started. Most of what I know about 3D, I learned on that project.

The ad became Log2Base2's flagship, earned the Gold Coin Award, and the character went on to front posters and seasonal campaigns.
